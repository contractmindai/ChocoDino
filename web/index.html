Privacy Policy for ChocoDino

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you play ChocoDino (the "Game").

1. Information We Collect

ChocoDino is designed as an offline, family-friendly game. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or precise location.

All game data is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to any external server. This includes:

  • Player progress (levels unlocked, stars earned)
  • In-game currency (points) and purchased items (skins, worlds)
  • Game settings and preferences (sound, notifications)

2. Device Permissions

The Game may request the following permissions for specific features:

  • Notifications: If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. These are generated by the app and do not involve a remote server.
  • Photo Gallery (Selfie Feature): When you choose to take a selfie with the dinosaur, the app accesses your device's photo gallery. This image is saved directly to your device and is not uploaded or shared by us.

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device's Settings.

3. Third-Party Services

ChocoDino does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with third parties.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you have full control over it. Uninstalling the Game from your device will permanently delete all your saved progress, settings, and local scores. We do not hold any copies of your data.

5. Children's Privacy

ChocoDino does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The game is safe for all ages and does not contain ads or external links.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
